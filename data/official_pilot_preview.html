Winners and Losers From AI Agents | Morgan Stanley

原題: Winners and Losers From AI Agents | Morgan Stanley

英語原文抜粋（翻訳未取得）

As AI agents become more capable of making decisions and purchases on consumers’ behalf, Morgan Stanley Research examines which industries could benefit—and which face growing competitive pressure. The recent launch of powerful consumer AI agents has reignited debate over how artificial intelligence could disrupt industries ranging from retail and insurance to transportation and live entertainment. Unlike traditional AI tools that primarily answer questions, these agents can browse websites, complete forms, compare prices, make purchases and even help negotiate bills. Morgan Stanley Research estimates that roughly $30 trillion in consumer spending could be digitized through AI agents across online advertising, e-commerce, travel, food delivery, ridesharing, logistics, wearables and other sectors. The extent of the disruption will depend on how quickly consumers adopt AI agents and how effectively companies make money from agent-driven activity and transactions.
