A backdoor Roth IRA is a strategy that some higher-income investors use to contribute to a Roth IRA by first making a nondeductible contribution to a traditional IRA and then converting those assets to a Roth IRA. Investors whose income exceeds the limits for direct Roth IRA contributions may consider a backdoor Roth IRA because Roth conversions are not subject to income restrictions.
